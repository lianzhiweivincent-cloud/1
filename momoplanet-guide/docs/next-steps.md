# Photos submitted. What next?

MomoPlanet creates your companion. The website and desktop app have different roles: submit photos and check progress on the website; use the ready companion in the app.

## 1. Check your own workspace

Return to your workspace through the [MomoPlanet homepage](https://momoplanet-ai.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=desktop_pet_guide_20261004). Check your pet's creation progress and follow the next step shown for your order.

A demonstration video cannot tell you whether your own companion is ready. Use your workspace status rather than guessing from elapsed time.

## 2. Install when your companion is ready

Enter Downloads from the official homepage. Choose the macOS download for a Mac or the Microsoft Store option for Windows, then follow the current installation instructions for your computer.

## 3. Sign in with the same account

In the desktop app, sign in with the MomoPlanet account that owns your pet. The ready companion syncs to the app automatically. **The first sync on a new device needs internet.**

If your companion is missing, first check the owning account and whether your workspace shows it as ready. For further help, use the official support route and describe the step where you got stuck: progress, installation, sign-in or synchronization.
