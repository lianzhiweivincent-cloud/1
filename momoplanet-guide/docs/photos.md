# Which photos should I use for a desktop pet?

Choose **at least three everyday photos of the same cat or dog**. Keep the set focused on one individual pet.

## Choose useful views

Look for clear photos that show your pet's features. A face view and another image showing more of the body can provide different information. Coat markings or the tail may also be helpful details.

These are selection tips. You do not need a fixed sequence of poses, mandatory camera angles or a professionally photographed set. Choose from the everyday photos you already have; the tips do not promise an exact result from any particular set.
